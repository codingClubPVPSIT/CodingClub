01 — The club

Curiosity is the starting point.
Everything else is built.

We are the Coding Club of PVPSIT’s Departments of CSE (AI&ML) and CSE (Data Science) — a space to take ideas apart, ask better questions, and build things that actually work.

Student-led. Department-backed.

A home for builders across AI&ML and Data Science.

Whether you are writing your first loop or shipping your tenth project, the club is designed for momentum. We pair peer learning with real challenges, so every session ends with something you can show.

  • ↳ Logical thinking challenges
